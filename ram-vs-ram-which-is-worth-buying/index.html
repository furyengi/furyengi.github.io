RAM vs Ram: Which One Is Actually Worth Buying?

A completely unserious comparison between the RAM in your PC and the ram in your yard: memory, speed, upkeep, price, and which one deserves your money.

Mahmud AbdulmalikOct 17, 2026 · 4 min read
Share on X

There are two things in my life that share a name, attract strong opinions, and make me wince when I see the price.

One of them lives inside your computer.

The other lives in a pen and is already staring at your neighbour's fence.

Both are called ram, only one of them is spelled in capital letters, and both will happily take your money.

So I decided to settle it properly. Let's compare them.

Round 1: Memory

RAM is volatile memory.

That is a polite way of saying it forgets everything the moment you cut the power. Close the laptop badly, lose the battery, trip over the cable, and every unsaved thing you were working on is gone.

A ram has the opposite problem.

A ram remembers everything. Who fed it. Who forgot to feed it. Who walked past it in a funny way in 2019.

Your PC will forget you. A ram will not.

Winner: the ram, but not always in a good way.

Round 2: Speed

Modern RAM moves data at speeds that are hard to even picture. Billions of operations a second, and it never once asks for a break.

A ram is also fast, but only in one situation: when you are the thing it is running toward.

Otherwise it stands in the middle of the road and takes its time.

Winner: RAM, by a mile. Though the ram wins the sprint at the exact moment you least want it to.

Round 3: Upkeep

RAM needs almost nothing. Slot it in, make sure the PC can see it, and forget about it. Maybe blow the dust out once in a while.

A ram needs food, water, shelter, a vet who picks up the phone, and someone to check on it every single day. Rain or no rain. Tired or not tired.

It will also make noise at hours that nobody agreed to.

Winner: RAM. Nobody has ever been woken up at 5 a.m. by a memory stick.

Round 4: Upgrades

With RAM, you check which slots are free, match the type, and add more. Dual channel is a real thing and it genuinely helps.

With a ram, you can also add more. But two rams in one space is not dual channel.

It is a boxing match.

And before anyone asks: no, you cannot download more RAM. You also cannot download more ram. We have tried.

Winner: RAM, unless you enjoy refereeing.

Round 5: Price

RAM prices go up and down depending on the market, and nobody can really explain why.

Ram prices go up and down depending on the season, and nobody has to explain why. Everyone already knows.

If you have ever shopped for a Sallah ram, you know the price conversation is a sport of its own, with rules, negotiation, drama and at least one person saying “last price” four times.

The difference is that when you buy RAM, the story ends at checkout.

When you buy a ram, the story is just starting.

Winner: RAM for your wallet. The ram for the stories.

Round 6: What happens when it fails

When RAM fails, you get a blue screen, random crashes, and a long evening of testing sticks one by one to find the bad one.

When a ram “fails”, it just does what it wants.

Honestly, it is the same energy. You will spend the evening trying to work out what went wrong, and the answer will not satisfy you.

Winner: a draw.

The developer angle

If you write software for a living, RAM is the thing your machine runs out of at the worst possible moment.

A hundred browser tabs, a few Docker containers, an editor, and a dev server you forgot you started yesterday. Then the laptop fan sounds like it is about to take off.

More RAM would solve that.

A ram would not solve that. A ram would make your standup meeting much more interesting, though.

The final verdict

If you need to run apps, compile code, edit video or keep an unreasonable number of tabs open, buy the RAM. It is cheaper to keep, easier to live with, and it will never headbutt your gate.

If you need a celebration, a farm, or a very loud new housemate, buy the ram.

And if you somehow need both, please keep them in separate rooms. One of them does not belong in a PC case, and the other does not belong in a pen.

I will let you work out which is which.
